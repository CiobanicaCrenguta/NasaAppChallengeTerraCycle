# Sources for the crop table

Every value in `crops.json` points to one of these ids. "Opened" says whether we read the figure in the source itself on 14 November 2026.

## S1

Ministerul Agriculturii și Dezvoltării Rurale (MADR); Academia de Științe Agricole și Silvice „Gheorghe Ionescu-Șișești” (ASAS). (2022). *Ghid de bune practici agricole privind efectele generate de schimbările climatice.* București: MADR.
URL: https://primariasieut.ro/documente/ghid-de-bune-practici-in-agricultura.pdf
Used for: sowing windows for rapeseed, wheat and barley; what rapeseed should not follow; wheat after wheat.
Opened: yes. Notes: the year is not printed; 2022 is inferred from the examples (agricultural year 2021–2022). This copy is hosted by a town hall. Press reports say the guide was published on madr.ro, but we did not find the original link there or on asas.ro. Page numbers are the printed ones.

## S2

URL: https://agronomyjournal.usamv.ro/pdf/2025/issue_2/Art76.pdf
Used for: best sowing window for winter wheat (Fundulea, 2022–2024).
Opened: yes.

## S3

Used for: sowing time of sunflower (Fundulea, 1990–1999).
Opened: yes.

## S4

Partal, E., Oltenacu, C. V., & Petcu, V. (2021). The influence of sowing date and plant density on maize yield and quality in the context of climate change in southern Romania. *Scientific Papers. Series A. Agronomy, 64*(1), 508–514.
URL: https://agronomyjournal.usamv.ro/pdf/2021/issue_1/Art67.pdf
Used for: sowing dates tested for maize (Fundulea, 2018–2020).
Opened: yes. Note: a trial result, not a recommendation, so the value stays `de_confirmat`.

## S5

Petcu, V., Stan, O., Băduț, C., Stanciu, V., & Bărbieru, A. (2020). Evaluarea unor genotipuri de soia pentru pretabilitatea la semănat timpuriu. *Analele I.N.C.D.A. Fundulea, 88*, 187–199.
URL: https://www.incda-fundulea.ro/anale/88/88.13.pdf
Used for: usual sowing time of soybean and the early sowing date tested.
Opened: yes. Note: the last page number was not legible in the file; check it before citing.

## S6

Institutul de Cercetare pentru Agricultură Ecologică (FiBL). (2022). *Cultura ecologică a plantelor oleaginoase* (Broșura nr. 1242, ediția pentru Republica Moldova). Frick: FiBL; Chișinău: Asociația Educație pentru Dezvoltare (AED).
URL: https://www.fibl.org/fileadmin/documents/shop/1242-plantelor-oleaginoase.pdf
Used for: years between sunflower crops and between rapeseed crops; gap between sunflower and other hosts of Sclerotinia.
Opened: yes. Notes: written for organic farming. Cited with the institute as author; the named authors are in the imprint.

## S9

Hatfield, J. L., & Prueger, J. H. (2015). Temperature extremes: Effect on plant growth and development. *Weather and Climate Extremes, 10*, 4–10. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.wace.2015.08.001

Opened: only the DOAJ record (title, authors, abstract). The full text was not read, so the values stay `de_confirmat`. The page range is from memory of the journal issue; check it before citing.

## S12

Raes, D., Steduto, P., Hsiao, T. C., & Fereres, E. (2026). *AquaCrop Version 7.3 Reference manual: Annexes.* Rome: Food and Agriculture Organization of the United Nations.
URL: https://www.fao.org/docs/devbuildingforwardbetterlibraries/default-document-library/annexesaquacropv73.pdf
Used for: the air temperature above which pollination starts to fail, for maize, wheat, sunflower, soybean and barley.
Opened: yes. Note: the values for maize, soybean and wheat are marked "Estimate" by the authors; those for sunflower and barley carry no such mark. Page numbers are those printed in Annex I.

## S13

Allen, R. G., Pereira, L. S., Raes, D., & Smith, M. (1998). *Crop evapotranspiration: Guidelines for computing crop water requirements* (FAO Irrigation and Drainage Paper 56), Chapter 6, Tables 11 and 12. Rome: Food and Agriculture Organization of the United Nations.
URL: https://www.fao.org/4/x0490e/x0490e0b.htm
Used for: crop coefficients (Kc) and lengths of growth stages.
Opened: yes. Note: stage lengths are given for a few regions of the world, none of them Romania; each value names the row used.

## S14

Food and Agriculture Organization of the United Nations. (n.d.). *ECOCROP: Database of crop environmental requirements.*
URL: https://gaez.fao.org/pages/ecocrop
Copy read: https://github.com/OpenCLIM/ecocrop/blob/main/EcoCrop_DB.csv
Used for: temperature, rain, soil pH and soil texture ranges, killing temperatures, cycle length.
Opened: the copy published by the OpenCLIM project, not FAO's own site. One range per species for the whole world; winter and spring types share one record.
